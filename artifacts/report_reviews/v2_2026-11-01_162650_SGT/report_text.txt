Abstract
This study forecasts same-session open-to-close returns for ten fixed current Nifty IT constituents from information aligned to the 08:55 IST decision clock. The canonical depth-constrained Extra Trees model combines own-stock, peer, U.S. IT-services, broad-market, macro and dated AI context. A completed strict-vintage sensitivity refits the exact specification after excluding all 60 fallback-flagged features and retains zero flagged inputs. The signed strategy applies a 15-bps effective round-trip hurdle and modeled turnover costs. In the 2025 historical replication it earns -4.04% net with 12.86% annualized volatility, Sharpe -0.26, and OOS R² versus the asset mean of -0.75%. In the separate January–September 2026 retrospective extension it earns 15.01%, with 13.74% volatility, Sharpe 1.46, and positive mean IC. The combined 10.37% path is dominated by the declining 2026 segment. The pooled AI-release effect is inconclusive, while the 2026 coefficient is negative after multiplicity adjustment; neither result identifies causality. The evidence supports a conditional defensive-timing hypothesis, not robust stock-selection alpha.
Principal result  Across the displayed 2025–September 2026 path, long exposure is active on 14 days, short exposure on 113 days, both sides on 0 days, and neither side on 307 days. Its value is defensive and conditional, not evidence for a deterministic AI-release short rule.

1. Research question and hypotheses
The primary question is whether completed U.S. and global information improves the decision to take a long, short, or flat position in current Nifty IT constituents at the Indian open. The secondary question is whether frontier-model releases or capability improvements contain incremental information about the subsequent returns of U.S. IT-services and Indian IT-services firms.
Six evidence questions organize the final report. H1–H6 below follow the saved hypothesis-analysis protocol; they replace the earlier draft numbering. Peer information and feature complexity remain controlled modeling comparisons in Sections 4–5. These questions were refined after inspecting historical results and are exploratory, not prospectively preregistered tests.

The fixed ridge experiment uses penalty 0.1 on identical stock/session observations across twenty quarters from 2021 to 2025. Average quarterly R² falls from approximately −0.7% for own history to −39.7% after adding raw S&P constituents and −55.2% for full context. These averages describe quarterly scores, not a pooled R². 4.2 AI event, parameter, and benchmark features
AI context is represented at three distinct evidentiary levels. Tradable event features use dated frontier releases and decaying pressure measures. Parameter features use disclosed total or active parameter counts only when explicitly reported and never treat missing disclosure as zero capability. Epoch benchmark scores are used for ex-post hypothesis analysis; current-vintage benchmark records are not injected into historical predictions. To avoid a visually uninformative inventory dump, the timeline marks only releases that establish a new running maximum in the mapped Epoch capability index. [6]

5.4 Signed strategy and evaluation

si,t ∈ {−1, 0, +1},   rp,tnet = Σi wi,t ri,tOC − c × turnovert
(3)
Forecast state and post-cost daily portfolio return.
The active signed rule uses 5 bps fee/spread proxy plus 2.5 bps slippage each way. The effective threshold is therefore max(5 bps configured forecast floor, 15 bps round-trip friction) = 15 bps. Up to three forecasts above +15 bps are long and up to three below −15 bps are short. Absolute stock weight is capped near one-third and total gross exposure at one. Positions enter from cash at the vendor open, exit at the close, and incur two-sided turnover equal to twice gross exposure. The +1/0/−1 states describe direction, not literal unit weights.

Grouped permutation diagnostics use the saved model results (Appendix E6–E7; supplementary plots described in Appendix D). Each repetition permutes a feature group by whole dates while preserving asset identities. Dispersion across repetitions and refits is descriptive variability, not a causal effect or a formal confidence interval.
Inference remains segment-aware. The 2025 HAC alpha test is inconclusive (p=0.887) and its block-bootstrap Sharpe interval spans negative and positive outcomes. The 2026 extension is stronger (HAC alpha p=0.303; Sharpe 1.46) but covers only 185 sessions in a pronounced decline. The combined curve is therefore an accurate return summary, not evidence that the same edge is stable in every regime.
Critical limitation  The audited official-provider calendar extends through 30 September 2026, but it is retrospective and sparse. The combined result evaluates a signed cross-market model, not a causal frontier-announcement trading rule.

7. Hypothesis evidence and regime diagnostics
7.1 Test definitions and multiplicity
The saved analysis aggregates observations by trading date rather than treating ten correlated stock rows as independent replications. H1 regresses Indian cohort intraday return on the available U.S. services return, lagged Indian return and lagged broad-market return. H2 replaces the U.S. term with the release indicator to avoid conditioning on a possible transmission channel. Newey–West HAC covariance uses five lags [19]. All estimable regression p-values in the saved family receive Holm step-down correction [20]; displayed confidence intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous. Circular date-block resampling uses 2,000 draws for H5 [21]. These controls do not erase prior researcher exposure to the sample. Analysis record: Appendix E13.

7.2 Cross-market association and event uncertainty
Because both return variables are in decimal units, the pooled H1 coefficient implies approximately +0.098 percentage points of Indian intraday return per +1 percentage point of the available U.S. return, conditional on the included lags. The 2026 coefficient is 0.181 (Holm p=0.0240), but the post-2024 change interaction is inconclusive (Holm p=1.000). A significant coefficient in one year and an insignificant coefficient in another is not itself a significant difference between years.

7.3 Selected-specification record across protocol stages
The selected specification returns +8.31% in 2021, +0.11% in 2022, +6.26% in 2023, +9.93% in 2024, −4.04% in the 2025 historical replication and +15.01% in the January–September 2026 extension. These values form one protocol history, not six independent tests: 2021–2024 influenced screening and confirmation, 2025 is an estimator-OOS historical replication after researcher exposure, and 2026 uses one fit on the declared rolling pre-2026 window, 25 November 2020–30 December 2025. The 2025 and 2026 bootstrap Sharpe intervals are wide and overlap zero. The full record therefore supports regime sensitivity and disciplined staged validation, not a claim of stable annual alpha.

7.5 H6: adaptation, staffing and credible counterevidence
Commercial adaptation is observable without assuming that AI necessarily improves margins. Infosys announced its Topaz generative-AI services offering on 23 May 2023 [9]. The Bank of England/FCA 2024 survey reports human oversight in semi-autonomous financial-services applications and only 2% of use cases with fully autonomous decision-making [10]. These sources support the relevance of enterprise integration and governance. They do not establish that Indian clients retained particular vendors because of cybersecurity, or that AI caused an equity recovery.
CX Dive reported on 9 May 2025 that Klarna was piloting recruitment of human customer-service staff while retaining its chatbot [11]. This is evidence of a hybrid support arrangement, not proof that the company abandoned AI or rehired a specified number of software developers. Conversely, Reuters reported renewed Indian IT disruption concerns on 5 February 2026, alongside analysts who considered the selloff excessive [12]. This counterexample prevents a one-directional narrative of early fear followed by universal acceptance.

8.2 Limitations
The fixed current-member cohort creates survivorship conditioning. Public vendor opens abstract from opening-auction queueing, bid–ask spread variation, taxes, borrow availability, short-sale constraints, market impact, and corporate-action edge cases. S&P and Nifty membership histories are public reconstructions rather than licensed exchange archives. The canonical model contains macro series whose histories were flagged as current-vintage fallbacks; the completed strict-vintage refit removes all 60 flagged columns and improves both return point estimates, but paired uncertainty remains wide. Feature exclusion addresses the known flags without proving perfect publication-time reconstruction for every remaining public series.
The effective inference sample is the number of serially dependent trading days, not the number of stock-day rows. AI releases are few, parameter disclosure is selective, parameter counts are not comparable across dense and mixture-of-experts architectures, and benchmark progress is multidimensional. The 2026 extension occurs during a sharp sector drawdown; its official-provider release calendar was reconstructed retrospectively through 30 September rather than maintained prospectively.
8.3 Conclusion
The strongest contribution is a temporally aligned, auditable retrospective design that converts cross-market forecasts into signed positions and reconciled costs. Extra Trees ranks highest under the configured historical confirmation rule, but the 2025 replication has weak forecast and net economic evidence: R² versus the asset mean and mean IC are negative, and costs more than eliminate the 2025 gross gain, taking +5.16% gross to −4.04% net. The 2026 extension is strongly positive during a sharp sector decline, making the combined result promising but regime-sensitive and dominated by defensive timing. The exact-specification strict-vintage sensitivity is complete: removing 60 flagged inputs improves both segment point estimates, yet paired intervals cross zero. Pooled AI-event evidence remains inconclusive; the negative 2026 association is exploratory rather than causal. The remaining evidentiary requirement is genuinely future observation after a prospective freeze.
